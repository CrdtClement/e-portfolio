Three profiles that inspire my plans :

Astou Lô

N7 engineer · Dual degree with Sciences Po · ENGIE Graduate Program

Interviewed by phone.

A dual degree opens doors for a technical profile. She told me this path is little known, yet Sciences Po offers more than thirty masters, so there is always one that fits.

The path is selective, requiring a clear first year at N7, internal approval, and thorough preparation for the entrance oral. During her Master's gap year at Sciences Po, she completed key internships that helped her build a strong network, directly leading to her first job at ENGIE.

What I take from it: Though she applies governance to energy while I target data, we share the same goal: making technology ethical. Combining N7’s technical depth with Sciences Po’s vision creates a strong hybrid advantage—the exact key to leading responsible tech transformations.

Supports: double degree, Sciences Po and Hertie School →

Victor Marti

N7 engineer (Digital Sciences) · DevOps and Cloud consultant at CGI

I contacted Victor on LinkedIn but have not received a reply yet, so this profile is based on his public information.

He studied in the same department as I do and spent an exchange semester at the Universidad Politécnica de Madrid (February to June 2023). He then worked as an AI market analyst intern before joining CGI, first as an intern and then as a consultant on cloud migration and CI/CD automation. He also won two national innovation hackathons with projects that included AI.

What I take from it: his work in data and network (Cloud, DevOps) represents exactly the kind of career path I am aiming for as a data enthusiast. At the same time, his semester in Madrid proves how an international exchange there fits into our department.

Supports: exchange at the Universidad Politécnica de Madrid →

Bastien Moussé

ENSIIE engineer · Quantitative risk at Forvis Mazars, Luxembourg

I contacted Bastien on LinkedIn but have not received a reply yet, so this profile is based on his public information.
