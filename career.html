Role models

Three profiles that inspire my plans, one for each mobility project.

Astou Lô

N7 engineer · Dual degree with Sciences Po · ENGIE Graduate Program

Interviewed by phone.

A dual degree opens doors for a technical profile. She told me this path is little known, yet Sciences Po offers more than thirty masters, so there is always one that fits.

The conditions are demanding: a first year at the N7 without any retake, the school's approval, then an oral exam at the end of the first master year that needs serious preparation. A gap year is possible but not compulsory.

What I take from it: her interest is sustainability, mine is data ethics, but the question is the same: what responsibilities come with a technology? I will aim for a first year without retakes and prepare the oral early.

Supports: double degree, Sciences Po and Hertie School →

Victor Marti

N7 engineer (Digital Sciences) · DevOps and Cloud consultant at CGI

I contacted Victor on LinkedIn but have not received a reply yet, so this profile is based on his public information.

He studied in the same department as I do and spent an exchange semester at the Universidad Politécnica de Madrid (February to June 2023). He then worked as an AI market analyst intern before joining CGI, first as an intern and then as a consultant on cloud migration and CI/CD automation. He also won two national innovation hackathons with projects that included AI.

What I take from it: a semester in Madrid fits well into a Digital Sciences career and leads to roles in data, AI and cloud engineering.

Supports: exchange at the Universidad Politécnica de Madrid →

Bastien Moussé

ENSIIE engineer · Quantitative risk at Forvis Mazars, Luxembourg

I contacted Bastien on LinkedIn but have not received a reply yet, so this profile is based on his public information.

ENSIIE is an engineering school comparable to the N7. He spent an exchange semester at the Politecnico di Milano (February to June 2025) in Mathematical Finance, with courses in econometrics, machine learning, time series and natural language processing. He then worked as a quantitative research intern and is now in quantitative risk.

What I take from it: his field is finance, not mine, but his experience shows that Milan offers strong data-related courses and that such an exchange leads to demanding technical careers.

Supports: exchange at the Politecnico di Milano →
