TIPE: Drawing with Fourier epicycles

C · SDL2 · Fourier series

The idea

Take the contour of an image and redraw it as the sum of rotating circles, called epicycles.

How it works

  1. Extract the contour of the image with the Moore neighbour tracing algorithm, written in C.
  2. Compute the Fourier coefficients of the contour.
  3. Sort the coefficients by modulus, so the largest circles come first.
  4. Animate the epicycles in real time with SDL2.

Key result

The regularity of the contour's derivative does not affect the quality of the reconstruction. The Gibbs phenomenon only appears on discontinuous contours.
