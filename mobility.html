My three International Mobility plans

Double degree: Sciences Po Paris and Hertie School

France and Germany · Exchange through a double degree

My most ambitious plan, if my first two years go well. A double degree with Sciences Po is not a mobility in itself, but it opens the door to one: as a student there, I would have access to its network of partner universities.

That is how I could spend an exchange semester at the Hertie School in Berlin, studying digital governance, algorithm ethics and data regulation in Europe.

Inspired by: Astou Lô, N7 engineer with a Sciences Po dual degree.

Universidad Politécnica de Madrid

Spain · Erasmus exchange

I am learning Spanish, and Madrid would be the ideal place to practise it. Computing and mathematics courses in the ETSIINF school would fit my data and AI interests, and the Erasmus programme keeps the process simple.

Studying there would also mean following courses in a new academic system and using Spanish every day, beyond the classroom. It is a way to turn a language I have just started into a real working skill, within Europe and close to home.

Inspired by: Victor Marti, N7 engineer (Digital Sciences) who spent an exchange semester there in 2023.

Politecnico di Milano

Italy · Study exchange

I have travelled to Italy twice, once in middle school through my Latin classes and once in high school with my theatre troupe, and I loved the country. An exchange at the Politecnico di Milano, a partner of ENSEEIHT, would let me study there and live in Italy rather than visit.

The courses are also a good fit: the public profile of an engineer who did his exchange there mentions econometrics, machine learning, time series and natural language processing, which are close to my interest in data.

Inspired by: Bastien Moussé, ENSIIE engineer who did an exchange semester there in 2025.

Why Europe

Europe appeals to me, and so does keeping the carbon footprint of my mobility low. There is no need to go far to gain knowledge and experience: the universities and companies I am targeting are close to home.

Languages: English (B2+ certified), Spanish (A1, currently learning).
