My three mobility plans

Double degree: Sciences Po Paris and Hertie School

France and Germany · Four-year path

My most ambitious plan, depending on how my first two years go: combine engineering with public policy of digital technology. More details below.

Universidad Politécnica de Madrid

Spain · Erasmus exchange

I am learning Spanish, and Madrid would be the ideal place to practise it. Computing and mathematics courses in the ETSIINF school would fit my data and AI interests, and the Erasmus programme keeps the process simple.

Inspired by: Victor Marti, N7 engineer (Digital Sciences) who spent an exchange semester there in 2023.

Politecnico di Milano

Italy · Study exchange

I have travelled to Italy twice, once in middle school through my Latin classes and once in high school with my theatre troupe, and I loved the country. An exchange at the Politecnico di Milano, a partner of ENSEEIHT, would let me study there and live in Italy rather than visit.

Inspired by: Bastien Moussé, ENSIIE engineer who did an exchange semester there in 2025.

Why Europe

Europe appeals to me, and so does keeping the carbon footprint of my mobility low. There is no need to go far to gain knowledge and experience: the universities and companies I am targeting are close to home.

Languages: English (B2+ certified), Spanish (A1, currently learning).

The double degree in four years

  1. Years 1–2, ENSEEIHT (Toulouse): engineering core of the Digital Sciences department. According to Astou Lô, the application requires a first year without any retake, the school's approval and the transcripts of the first master year, the third year of the engineering cycle and my last year of preparatory classes. An oral exam at the end of the first master year then needs serious preparation. Ideally, I would also do my second-year internship abroad (8 to 10 weeks).
  2. Year 3, Sciences Po Paris : a master adapted to my profile, for example in public affairs with a focus on digital technology and public policy, or in international affairs with a technology focus.
  3. Year 4, semester 1, Hertie School, Berlin : exchange focused on digital governance, algorithm ethics and data regulation in Europe.
  4. Year 4, semester 2, final-year internship, Paris : six months in a tech consultancy, a regulator, a large company or a ministry.
